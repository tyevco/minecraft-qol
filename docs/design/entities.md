# Entities — Concept Sheet

**Custom entities that expand play without trivialising it**

Target: `@minecraft/server` 2.9.0 / `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.1.0 · Entity format version 1.26.40 · Draft v0.1

> Brainstorm, not a build order. Each concept has a model and atlas generated
> into `concepts/entities/` (see `concepts/README.md`) so it can be judged in
> the viewer, and a "must prototype" list naming what the probe pack has to
> measure before a design doc is written. Nothing here has been run in game.

---

## 1. The stance

The packs so far remove friction (QOL Times, Fluidworks, Graves, Hearthstone)
or make the world safer for the kids (Guardian, Lens, Bulwark). An entity that
does a job *for* the player is a different thing: it is easy to build one that
makes the game boring. Every concept below therefore carries a cost that keeps
the player in the loop:

- **It can be hurt.** Nothing here is invulnerable; the decoy breaks, the mule
  is a target, the runner has to be fed.
- **It has range.** Companions work near the player or near a home block, not
  across the map.
- **It is slower than doing it yourself, once.** A runner beats walking a mob
  farm's floor for the hundredth time; it does not beat picking up one drop.
- **It is opt-in per role** through the settings panel, so a kid who does not
  want a raid never gets one.

Two things were considered and rejected because they cross that line: a
lamplighter golem that torches dark spots (Lens with the player removed) and
any mobile ranged attacker (Bulwark is stationary on purpose).

## 2. What the stable API gives an entity

Checked against the installed `node_modules/@minecraft/server/index.d.ts`,
following the corrections in `docs/README.md`.

| Need | Stable mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | `EntityTameableComponent.tameToPlayer` / `tamedToPlayerId`; the id is a player dynamic property, never `persistentId` (beta) |
| Carrying items | `minecraft:inventory` on the entity; `EntityInventoryComponent.container` in script |
| Following, fetching, patrolling | Vanilla goals: `follow_owner`, `pickup_items`, `go_and_give_items_to_owner`, `home` + `move_towards_home_restriction`, `nearest_attackable_target` |
| Interaction | `playerInteractWithEntity` before/after events; the held stack is on the event |
| Damage numbers, hit reactions | `entityHurt`, `entityHitEntity`, `projectileHitEntity` |
| Growth and variants | Component groups swapped by `triggerEvent`; `minecraft:scale`; an int property with `client_sync` driving the texture, as `bulwark:tier` does |
| Persistence | `minecraft:persistent` plus a block anchor and reconciliation where the entity must survive (the Bulwark pattern) |
| Leading, riding | `minecraft:leashable`, `minecraft:rideable`; `EntityLeashableComponent`, `EntityRideableComponent` |

Not available, and the concepts are shaped around it: making vanilla mobs
*avoid* a custom entity (avoidance lives on the mob's own AI), reading a
tamed pet's variant back from script for re-spawning (read-only), and block
entities (still experimental), so any per-entity config that must survive a
chunk unload goes in a dynamic property on the entity or in the position index.

## 3. The concepts

Models are under `concepts/entities/models/`, atlases under
`concepts/entities/textures/`, both generated by `tools/`. All face -z. Bones
are split where an animation would move them, so a chosen concept can be
animated without re-authoring.

### 3.1 Decoy dummy — Bulwark

A scarecrow in the `player` type family so zombies, skeletons and creepers
target it instead of the player. It has health, it breaks, and it is repaired
by interacting with hay or wool. In a Bulwark kill zone it is a trap that is
fun to build and still needs upkeep.

The same entity is a **training dummy**: on `entityHurt` the damage is flashed
in its name tag, so weapons can be compared.

- Model: stake, burlap body with a painted bullseye, straw hands, sack head.
  `body` and `head` are separate bones so a hit can rock them; `chest` locator
  for a puff of straw.
- **Must prototype:** whether `is_family: player` on a custom entity satisfies
  vanilla hostile target filters at all; whether a creeper's swell triggers
  against it; whether `damage_sensor` can take the hit without the entity
  being knocked around; how the kids react to a thing that looks like a
  player being hit.

### 3.2 Patrol golem — Bulwark

Bulwark's mobile sibling. A melee golem bound to a home block with a patrol
radius, on the vanilla `home` component. It costs iron to build and iron to
heal, and the home block is its persistence anchor, reusing Bulwark's
block-to-entity pairing and reconciliation.

- Model: stone limbs, iron chest plate, boots and shoulder caps, an iron cap
  above lit eye slits. Legs and arms on their own bones for a walk cycle;
  `head` yaws with `look_at_target`; `eyes` locator.
- **Must prototype:** the Bulwark probe first (`docs/bulwark-turret-probe.md`),
  since it answers the persistence and reconciliation questions for both;
  whether `home` keeps a `navigation.walk` entity inside a radius reliably
  across chunk reloads; melee `on_kill` routing (it is in the fixed list,
  unlike `ranged_attack`).

### 3.3 Runner — companions

A clockwork fetcher in Fluidworks copper. It hovers, picks up drops near it
with `pickup_items`, and hands them to its owner with
`go_and_give_items_to_owner`. Tidy's item magnet with a body and a range
limit. Fed with copper to keep working, so it is a pet rather than a vacuum.

The **Quartermaster** is the same entity with a different job: keeping Bulwark
turrets fed from a supply chest. Bulwark's ammo rule stays; the runner only
shortens the walk.

- Model: a copper drum with a glass front (the carried item renders at the
  `hand` locator and shows through it), a lensed head with an antenna bulb,
  two iron fan blades on `left_wing` / `right_wing`, pincers underneath,
  `exhaust` locator for steam.
- **Must prototype:** whether the two allay goals work on a non-allay entity
  with `navigation.hover`; whether a custom entity can push into a hopper by
  script, or the turret has to accept an interaction instead; what happens
  to a held item on `/reload` and on chunk unload.

### 3.4 Hatchling — companions

A pure fun pet for the kids. Tamed from an egg, it grows through scale tiers
over real play sessions, takes a name, and never fights. Three variants
(ember, moss, frost) are a palette swap of one atlas, like the turret tiers,
picked by an entity property. Pairs with Guardian Phase 3 pet insurance.

- Model: cat-sized, scaled body with a plated belly, snouted head with horn
  nubs, four legs, two-segment tail with a spade, wing buds tilted off the
  flanks. Every limb is a bone; `mouth` locator for a puff of smoke, spores
  or frost per variant.
- **Must prototype:** whether `minecraft:scale` swapped by component group
  survives a reload without the entity popping; the tame-from-egg flow on
  `playerInteractWithEntity` with an offline owner; whether the variant
  property round-trips through `setProperty`.

**The egg** is its own entity, `concept_egg`, so it can sit anywhere, wobble,
and be picked back up. The item places it; it hatches after real play
sessions near its owner rather than on a timer, so leaving the Realm running
overnight does nothing. Interacting with it warms it (a spark, a moss cutting
or an ice shard by variant) and advances an int property `concept:cracks`
(0 to 2). At 2 the next warming sets `concept:hatching`; the hatch animation
plays, the egg removes itself and spawns the hatchling of the same variant,
tamed to whoever warmed it last.

- Model: an egg stacked from five cubes on a straw nest, one atlas per
  variant sharing the hatchling's palettes. `crack_1` and `crack_2` are
  alpha-tested overlay cubes a quarter pixel proud of the shell, each side
  reading its own quadrant of the crack tile so the cracks differ around the
  egg; a render controller shows them by `concept:cracks`. `top` locator for
  the hatch burst.
- **Must prototype:** `part_visibility` on a render controller driven by an
  entity property (the vanilla shape, but not yet used in this repo);
  whether an `entity_alphatest` overlay a quarter pixel proud z-fights with
  the shell at distance; that `spawnEntity` from the egg's own `entityRemove`
  handler is allowed, or whether the hatch has to be a script-scheduled
  remove-then-spawn.

### 3.5 Messenger — companions

Interact holding an item, pick a player from a form, and the bird flies off.
The item lands in that player's mailbox block after a delay scaled by
distance. The bird is real for the first fifty blocks then despawns; the
delivery is a scheduled dynamic-property record, so it survives unloads.
Not instant, can be shot, one stack at a time. Good for a family that plays
at different times.

- Model: a pigeon with a buckled satchel on its chest, folded wings on their
  own bones, a barred tail, beak and legs; `letter` locator.
- **Must prototype:** `navigation.fly` towards a script-set target on a
  passive entity; whether a despawned carrier's stack can be held only in a
  world dynamic property without loss (Graves' "fail towards the player"
  rule applies: the stack must be recoverable if the mailbox is gone).

### 3.6 Pack mule — companions

A tameable follower with a real inventory. Lead it home and interact with a
marked chest to unload it. It walks, it can be hurt, and it despawns if it
wanders without `minecraft:persistent`. Ties into Fluidworks' Collector as
the "bring the harvest home" step.

- Model: vanilla horse proportions, a long body on solid legs, the neck
  pitched forward by bone rotation so the head follows it, a boxy skull with
  a longer muzzle, ears, eyes on the cheeks and a blaze on the forehead; a
  leather harness, and a burlap pannier with a leather flap each side on
  `left_pack` / `right_pack` so an empty side can be hidden by bone visibility.
- **Must prototype:** `minecraft:inventory` plus `leashable` on one entity;
  whether `EntityInventoryComponent.container` is readable while the entity
  is being led; unload into a `BlockInventoryComponent` chest with the
  plan-then-mutate shape from Fluidworks' `execute`.

## 4. Animations

Each concept has an animation set and a controller under
`concepts/entities/animations/` and `animation_controllers/`, generated by
`tools/animations/generate.ts` against the bones the model generator wrote,
so an animation cannot name a bone that does not exist. The viewer plays
them: pick the controller to watch the state machine react to the "moving"
and "in the air" switches, or a single animation to loop it.

| Concept | Loops | One-shot | Controller states |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decoy | `idle` sways on the stake | `hit` rocks body and head back, then settles (0.5 s) | idle → hit on `query.hurt_time > 0` |
| Patrol golem | `idle` scans; `walk` swings legs and arms in opposition, body rises per step | `attack` overhead slam with the right arm (0.6 s) | idle ↔ walk on move speed; attack on `variable.attack_time` |
| Runner | `hover` bobs with a fast wing beat; `dart` leans into travel | `deliver` a nod as it hands over (0.8 s) | hover ↔ dart; deliver on `query.property('concept:delivering')` |
| Hatchling | `idle` breathes, wags, glances; `walk` trots | `flap` a hop with two wing beats when fed (0.6 s) | idle ↔ walk; flap on `query.property('concept:happy')` |
| Messenger | `idle` pigeon head-jerks (3.2 s keyframes); `walk` bobs; `fly` beats wings, tucks legs | — | idle / walk on move speed; fly on `!query.is_on_ground` |
| Pack mule | `idle` tail swish, head dips; `walk` four-beat with the panniers swinging against the stride | `graze` lowers the neck (2.4 s) | idle ↔ walk; graze on `query.property('concept:grazing')` |
| Hatchling egg | `idle` barely breathes; `wobble` rocks in two bursts per 2.4 s once cracked | `hatch` squash, stretch and shudder (1.2 s) | idle ↔ wobble on `concept:cracks`; hatch on `concept:hatching` |

Conventions, stated in the generator: walk cycles are driven by
`query.modified_distance_moved` scaled by `query.modified_move_speed`, as
vanilla does, so stride matches ground covered and fades at rest; idle motion
runs on `query.life_time` so it never restarts on a state change; one-shots
end on `query.all_animations_finished`. Script cannot set Molang variables,
so the one-shots a pack would fire are gated on a bool entity property.

**Must prototype:** `variable.attack_time` for the golem is the vanilla iron
golem's convention and may need a `pre_animation` line to copy it from
`query.attack_time`; whether `query.hurt_time` is nonzero for a
`damage_sensor` that cancels damage (the decoy takes hits it should not
suffer); the `blend_transition` values, which the viewer does not preview.

## 5. Also considered

- **Pet whistle** — an item, not an entity: teleports loaded tamed animals to
  the player. Belongs to Guardian Phase 3.
- **Raid bell** — a block that calls a short hostile wave to the base for a
  drop at the end. The reason to want the dummy and the golem. Opt-in per role.
- **The Tinker** — a custom trader with data-driven trade tables selling pack
  items for mob drops, as a progression gate. Visits the Hearthstone.
- **Cart** — a rideable, leashable chest on wheels. Cosmetic, and the kind of
  thing the kids talk about.

## 6. Suggested order

1. **Decoy dummy.** Smallest entity, no pathing, one probe to answer, and it
   turns Bulwark's kill zone into a build goal.
2. **Runner.** Vanilla goals do the work, and it de-risks the Quartermaster
   and the Messenger.
3. **Hatchling**, once Guardian's pet phase lands, so insurance covers it.

Each becomes its own design doc when picked up, with the probe results in a
`docs/*-results.md` first.
